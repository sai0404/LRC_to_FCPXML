# LRC_to_FCPXML
Make your Lyrical video songs from given lrc ( lyrics with time-synced) singing content perfectly.I'm presently made only for final cut pro where you can download your time-synced generator(normal one) by giving input as video's .lrc or .srt version.
**User guide:**
**step 1:** As per the video, prepare the sound's .lrc or .srt version.(contins time stamps with scripts)
**step 2:**Upload that file(lrc/srt) and click the button(convert to fcpxml)
**Eg:**Lrc: [00:00:00] start
            [00:04:00] vocals
                  .      .
                  .      .
                  .      .
            [00:07:00] end
**step 3:**Download the file or copy and save the content using .fcpxml extension to the file.
**step 4:**Final_cutPro->file->import->XML->select this .fcpxml file.
**step 5:** *== Success ==*
